Design philosophy

Architecture is an act of service to every person who moves through a space: the technician in the service corridor, the family in its first home and the guest arriving at a suite. Their paths never cross. That is design.
Circulation first

How people enter, move, pause and leave is studied before a single wall is drawn. Every user gets a route that respects them.

Designing for the senses

Colour, light, texture, acoustics and scent are design materials. Calm tones where people rest, energy where they cook, eat and work.

Responsible by default

Site-responsive orientation, less waste and responsible specification, built for the community and the environment it sits in.

Wellbeing as the measure

Scale and budget are inputs. Every project is measured by the quality of life it returns to the people inside it.

How design management is run

One governed path from feasibility to as-built

The Design Management Office established at MENA governs every project through the same six stages, with a named owner and a recorded decision at each.

  1. Feasibility

    Site analysis, GFA optimisation and planning constraints for new acquisitions, presented to senior leadership.

  2. Concept & design

    Architectural identity, interior mood boards and the brief, aligned with Marketing, Finance and Procurement.

  3. IFC

    Multi-trade coordination of structure, MEP, architecture and interiors; conflicts resolved at documentation stage.

  4. Shop drawings

    Every technical submission reviewed and approved through the shop-drawing log and approval workflow.

  5. Construction

    Construction-phase technical rulings, and the design-to-procurement cycle for finishing and specialist packages.

  6. As-built

    As-built verification, so the handed-over building matches the approved design.
